How to Use This Studio
  1. Enter the Featured Cat name.
  2. Click Cat Photo and choose the photo you want to use.
  3. Use Cat Size and Cat Position to place the cat correctly with the magician's hat and wand.
  4. Enter the Winner, Prize, and Drawing Date.
  5. Click Preview Drawing and watch the entire reel.
  6. Make any necessary corrections to the photo placement, names, prize, or date.
  7. Click Record & Download Reel.
  8. When the browser asks what you want to share, choose This Tab.
  9. Let the reel play all the way through. The finished video will download automatically.
  10. Save the downloaded video in the appropriate folder and it is ready to post, send, or share.

Important: Always run one complete preview before recording. Cat photos are cropped differently, so the size and position may need to be adjusted for each photo.

While recording: Do not close the tab or navigate away while the button says Recording.... Wait for the video to finish and download automatically.
