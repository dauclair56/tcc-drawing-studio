How to Use This Studio

Daily Workflow

  1. If applicable, open the winner from the Calendar Raffle Drawing Manager.
    After the winner has been confirmed, click Open in Drawing Studio. The winner's name, prize, and drawing date will automatically appear in this Studio.
  2. Choose the Featured Cat.
    Enter the cat's name and select the cat's photo for today's drawing.
  3. Adjust the cat photo.
    Use Cat Size and Cat Position to make sure the cat looks good in the drawing.
  4. Check the drawing information.
    Confirm that the winner's name, prize, and drawing date are correct.
  5. Preview the drawing.
    Click Preview Drawing to watch the complete drawing before recording it.
  6. Record the reel.
    When everything looks right, click Record & Download Reel. The Studio will play and record the complete drawing and automatically download the finished video.

Customize Wording

Use Customize Wording if you want to change any of the standard text that appears during the drawing. These changes affect the wording of the reel, not the winner information.

Important While Recording

Once recording begins, do not close the tab, switch away from the Studio, or start another drawing while the button says Recording.... Wait until the drawing finishes and the video downloads automatically.
